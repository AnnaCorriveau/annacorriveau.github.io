I am a cognitive psychologist interested in **dynamic attention and cognition** and how these internal states impact how we **maintain and execute behaviors**, including complex actions. I use neuroimaging and behavioral methods to probe moment-to-moment changes in attentional state with the goal of answering the following questions: what underlies these fluctuations and what are the consequences for how we perceive, remember, and act?

I am also an advocate for **transparent and open science** and believe science should be accessible to everyone. I previously lead the University of Chicago chapter of the [ReproducibiliTea Journal Club](https://reproducibilitea.org/), an initiative aimed at increasing scientific reproduciblity, transparency, and rigor. You can also find all of my research materials on the [Open Science Framework website](https://osf.io/profile/).
